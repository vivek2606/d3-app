3. Bulk export

Loops are named Song_Loop01_120BPM_4Bar.wav: 16-bit PCM, bar-exact length, start snapped to a zero crossing, micro-fades and an embedded loop point. Todas are whole bars (Song_Toda01_120BPM_1Bar.wav) and rolls are whole beats (Song_Roll01_120BPM_2Beat.wav), so they stretch in sync. They have no loop point and play once per trigger.
